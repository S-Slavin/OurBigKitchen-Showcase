# OurBigKitchen VMS - Grant Application Strategy
*Prepared for Mimi Ochoa - Grant Applications*

## Executive Summary

OurBigKitchen is developing a comprehensive Volunteer Management System (VMS) mobile app that directly addresses critical challenges in food rescue operations and volunteer coordination. This digital platform is specifically designed to increase volunteer engagement, improve retention, and scale food rescue capacity across NSW and Australia.

## What We Want the App to Do - Detailed Vision

### **Core Mission:**
Transform how food rescue organizations manage volunteers by providing a comprehensive digital platform that streamlines every aspect of volunteer coordination while ensuring compliance, tracking impact, and building community engagement.

### **Primary Functions:**

**1. Volunteer Registration & Onboarding:**
- **Streamlined Sign-up**: Mobile-first registration that takes volunteers from interest to active participation in under 5 minutes
- **Compliance Management**: Integrated WWCC verification for 18+ volunteers with automated renewal reminders
- **Volunteer Type Support**: Individual volunteers and corporate volunteer programs with different workflows
- **Health & Safety**: Built-in health protocols, safety guidelines, and terms acceptance tracking

**2. Event & Session Management:**
- **Smart Scheduling**: Automated volunteer scheduling based on availability, skills, and event requirements
- **Real-time Coordination**: Live updates on volunteer check-ins, check-outs, and session status
- **Event Creation**: Organizations can create and manage food rescue events with specific volunteer needs
- **Capacity Planning**: Predictive analytics for volunteer requirements based on event size and type

**3. Impact Tracking & Measurement:**
- **Comprehensive Metrics**: Track meals prepared, people served, volunteer hours, food waste reduced, and environmental impact
- **Real-time Dashboards**: Live updates for organizations and volunteers on their collective impact
- **Environmental Calculations**: Automatic carbon footprint reduction and food waste prevention metrics
- **Social Sharing**: Built-in sharing of impact achievements to amplify community engagement

**4. Volunteer Engagement & Retention:**
- **Progress Tracking**: Volunteers can see their personal impact and contribution history
- **Achievement System**: Recognition for milestones, hours contributed, and community impact
- **Communication Tools**: In-app messaging and updates to keep volunteers informed and engaged
- **Community Building**: Social features that connect volunteers and build lasting relationships

**5. Administrative Efficiency:**
- **Automated Workflows**: Reduce manual processes for volunteer coordination
- **Reporting & Analytics**: Comprehensive reports on volunteer engagement, impact metrics, and operational efficiency
- **Multi-organization Support**: Platform can serve multiple food rescue organizations simultaneously
- **Integration Ready**: API endpoints for connecting with existing systems and databases

## Current App Capabilities vs. Future Vision

- **User Authentication**: Secure login and account management
- **Onboarding System**: Custom welcome slides explaining community impact

### **What We Want to Build (Future Vision):**
